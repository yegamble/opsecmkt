Bitcoin escrow recovery and reconciliation ({{template "btc-escrow-chain" $.EscrowNet}})

{{with $.NoticeIn "escrow-recovery"}}
{{.}}
{{end}}{{if .Gate}}
Bitcoin escrow paused for restore reconciliation. While the recovery lock is set the escrow watcher builds, co-signs and broadcasts no escrow settlement; it still reads deposits and follows broadcast settlements to their confirmations. Clearing the recovery lock (see the recovery runbook) resumes building and co-signing; a settlement held by the restore still waits for its check below.
{{end}}

A restore from backup holds every restored escrow settlement that has a stored final transaction: the restored records may predate its broadcast, or a transaction that replaced it. While the recovery lock is set, every stored transaction waits too. Check each one on the chain and in the escrow watch-only wallet (gettransaction of its transaction ID, gettxout of its inputs), then confirm it: the market broadcasts it once, and while the recovery lock is set it does not send it again. One the watch-only wallet shows on the network is recorded as broadcast without being sent, and one that confirms is released by itself. Each confirmation needs your password and is audited.

Missing Monero escrow wallets past their destruction date

A wallet the escrow wallet-rpc cannot open is missing, and the market keeps its sealed seed: its file may be on a volume not yet restored, and it may still hold coins. Once you have checked that its files are gone from the escrow wallet volume and its backups, and that nothing in it is owed, confirm its destruction: its sealed seed is wiped and it can never be recovered. You cannot confirm a wallet holding an order of yours. Each confirmation needs your password and is audited.

Destroyed Monero escrow wallets

Each escrow wallet is destroyed 90 days after its last order closed, once nothing is left in it: its password is replaced by one never kept and its sealed seed wiped, so the market can never open it again. The market cannot reach the escrow wallet volume: delete each wallet's two files, NAME and NAME.keys, from it yourself. Backups of that volume keep copies until they expire.
