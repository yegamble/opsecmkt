Test networks only; the server refuses to start against mainnet and disables a provider whose node later reports mainnet. An unreachable node or a wallet that is not loaded does not stop the site: that currency shows as unavailable, payment requests for it are refused, and the check is retried every poll. Deposits sit in one pooled wallet per currency. There is no commission or fee accounting.

New payment addresses

Enable or pause Bitcoin and Monero separately. Pausing prevents new payment addresses; existing deposit addresses, payment confirmation, refunds and payouts continue. Wallet RPC connections must be provisioned on the server first. Changes require your password and are audited.

Each payout is attempted once. Failed or stuck payouts are never retried automatically: check the wallet before paying anything manually. A payout for which the wallet reported a pre-broadcast error was not broadcast; one whose outcome is unknown (the wallet call timed out or its reply was lost) or that is stuck in sending may have been. After checking the wallet you can release a held payout, requeue a payout that was not broadcast (confirming that explicitly when the outcome was unknown), or record one the wallet shows as sent. Each action needs your password and is audited.
