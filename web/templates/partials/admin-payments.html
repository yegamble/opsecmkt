Held releases of banned vendors

{{with $.NoticeIn "ban-hold-refund-days"}}
{{.}}
{{end}}

In force: {{.Days}} days. A release to a vendor whose account is banned is held. Once it has been held this long with the ban still in place (counted from the ban, or from when the order completed or its dispute was decided, whichever is later; a new ban starts again), an administrator who is not a party may refund that order's escrow to the buyer's refund address given at checkout instead, one order at a time from its order page. That includes orders the buyer confirmed and completed digital orders the vendor never claimed. Nobody else is ever paid.

Applies at once to every held release, including those already held. A database restore rolls this setting back to its value at the backup. {{template "confirm-fields" (and $.Security $.Security.TOTPEnabled)}}

Missing Monero escrow wallets past their destruction date

A wallet the escrow wallet-rpc cannot open is missing, and the market keeps its sealed seed: its file may be on a volume not yet restored, and it may still hold coins. Once you have checked that its files are gone from the escrow wallet volume and its backups, and that nothing in it is owed, confirm its destruction: its sealed seed is wiped and it can never be recovered. You cannot confirm a wallet holding an order of yours. Each confirmation needs your password and is audited.

Destroyed Monero escrow wallets

Each escrow wallet is destroyed 90 days after its last order closed, once nothing is left in it: its password is replaced by one never kept and its sealed seed wiped, so the market can never open it again. The market cannot reach the escrow wallet volume: delete each wallet's two files, NAME and NAME.keys, from it yourself. Backups of that volume keep copies until they expire.
