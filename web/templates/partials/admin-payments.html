A payout for which the wallet reported a pre-broadcast error was not broadcast; one whose outcome is unknown (the wallet call timed out or its reply was lost) or that is stuck in sending may have been. Saving a payout address never moves a payout that already has one: you can move a held payout, or one the wallet definitely rejected, to the recipient's current saved address after checking that address with the account owner. A payout that is being sent or may have been broadcast is never moved. Each action needs your password and is audited.
