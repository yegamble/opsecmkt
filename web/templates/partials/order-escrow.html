Bitcoin escrow REGTEST

Regtest only. Bitcoin escrow on this market runs on regtest, a local test chain, and has not been verified on any public network; its coins have no value. Coins leave an escrow only by a settlement the market builds: the vendor's claim, a refund, a dispute decision or the recovery of coins sent after the order closed. Once the buyer or the vendor has signed one in their own wallet, the market co-signs it and broadcasts it to the regtest network.
