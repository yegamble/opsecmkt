Bitcoin escrow on this market runs on {{template "btc-escrow-net" $.EscrowNet}}, and has not been verified on any public network; its coins have no value. Coins leave an escrow only by a settlement the market builds: the vendor's claim, a refund, a dispute decision or the recovery of coins sent after the order closed. Once the buyer or the vendor has signed one in their own wallet, the market co-signs it and broadcasts it to the {{template "btc-escrow-network" $.EscrowNet}}; when both have signed it, the market does not sign it and broadcasts what they signed.{{if .PartiesOnly}} The release to the vendor of a shipped order is different: it needs both the vendor's and the buyer's signatures, and the market never signs it before the order is completed.{{end}}
