Image scanner

Every photo and avatar upload is checked by the ClamAV malware scanner before anything is stored; only clamd’s answer that it found nothing (stream: OK) lets it through. It reports the scanner’s state to this app over its private connection about every 30 seconds; this panel shows the latest report. This application does not contact ClamAV or receive original files.{{else}}This panel shows what the app last heard from the scanner, checked every 30 seconds.{{end}}
