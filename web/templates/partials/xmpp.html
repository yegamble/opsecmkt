XMPP notifications

Receive the same alerts as your market notifications at your private XMPP address: orders, messages and dispute updates. Staff receive only alerts their current role permits.

XMPP sends notification text outside the market. Your XMPP provider can see it and associate the destination with market activity. Notification summaries are not end-to-end encrypted. Message contents are excluded unless you separately choose to include already PGP-encrypted messages below. The market never decrypts them. Delivery depends on the operator enabling outgoing XMPP; your market notifications stay available if delivery fails.

Use an address without a /resource. This destination is shown only in your account preferences. Changing it or disabling delivery cancels queued alerts for the previous preference; earlier notifications are not sent when you opt in.

{{with .XMPPAccount}}{{if .ContentsUnavailable}}{{.ContentsUnavailable}} {{end}}{{end}}This separate choice requires XMPP notifications to be enabled and a usable PGP encryption key. Clear both choices to turn off XMPP. When the administrator allows it, new encrypted messages addressed to you can include their PGP message block in XMPP, with the order reference when one exists. Plaintext messages are never forwarded. Keep the corresponding private key in your own PGP client to decrypt the block. XMPP services can retain the ciphertext and notification metadata.
