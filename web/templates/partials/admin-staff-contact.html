Shown on the Transparency page, to a suspended account signing in, to someone who lost their sign-in factors or whose PGP key expired, beside the CAPTCHA and under notifications that tell people to contact the market staff. Write a handle, onion address or XMPP address. With the CAPTCHA on, use an address that works without an account: a bare handle can be messaged only from an account, which someone who cannot read the CAPTCHA image cannot get. It is shown as text, never as a link; when it is exactly the handle of a moderator or administrator whose key can receive encrypted messages, signed-in users also get a link to message that handle. While it is empty, those places say “Ask the operator of this market.”

At most 200 characters on one line. Anyone can read it, including visitors who are not signed in. Save it empty to remove it.
