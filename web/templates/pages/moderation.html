Moderation budget

Lifetime allocations
{{.AllocatedRecords}} used of {{.MaxRecords}}
Reserved bytes
{{.AllocatedMiB}} MiB used of {{.MaxMiB}} MiB ({{.ByteAllocations}} allocations)

Each allocation takes one lifetime slot and reserves 512 KiB (524288 bytes), so 1 MiB holds two allocations. Capture stops when either limit runs out; at the defaults the byte limit binds first, so raise both. Raising the allocations also raises each account's daily share (2% of it, at least 8).

This form only raises the budget. It takes effect at once: targets waiting for capture are captured on the worker's next pass, without a restart. A worker flag below the stored value is ignored. Lowering the budget is a host-only procedure.
