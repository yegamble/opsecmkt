Each allocation reserves 524288 bytes for a bounded job and its future audit records. The default byte limit allows 512 lifetime allocations. These limits do not bound physical disk, indexes, WAL or backups. Completed allocations remain reserved; no history is purged automatically.

New captures retain references and hashes. The worker reads matching live content in memory; source changes default to HOLD. Historical legacy copies remain under their existing disclosure. Completed reference captures are skipped by archived replay.

Each allocation takes one lifetime slot and reserves 512 KiB (524288 bytes), so 1 MiB holds two allocations. Capture stops when either limit runs out; at the defaults the byte limit binds first, so raise both. Raising the allocations also raises each account's daily share (2% of it, at least 8).

This form only raises the budget. It takes effect at once: targets waiting for capture are captured on the worker's next pass, without a restart. A worker flag below the stored value is ignored. Lowering the budget is a host-only procedure.
