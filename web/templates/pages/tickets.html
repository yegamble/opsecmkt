Open an encrypted ticket

Tickets have no plaintext subject. New tickets and their metadata are visible to moderators and administrators. Choosing an administrator as the recipient does not escalate the ticket or restrict moderator access. The message body stays PGP encrypted to you and the selected recipient. Confirm key fingerprints through a trusted channel before encrypting.
