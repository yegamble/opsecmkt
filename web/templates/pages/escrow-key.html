The wallet the key comes from

With a per-order wallet you make a new wallet for each order from the same seed, on a path the market gives for that order. Electrum's own seed type, the one Electrum (also on Tails) makes by default, cannot do this: it fixes the multisig path, so it cannot follow a per-order path. You need a BIP39 seed (in Electrum, tick BIP39 seed under Options when you enter the seed) or Sparrow. Register that wallet's native SegWit multisig account key, on the path m/48h/1h/0h/2h. Escrows with per-order wallets are not available on this market yet; you can register the key and sign its practice transaction now. These steps have not yet been tried with a real Electrum or Sparrow wallet.

Registering a per-order key replaces the Bitcoin Core key this account uses now. Until escrows with per-order wallets are built, that stops Bitcoin escrow buying, and for a vendor under escrow-only intake it stops Bitcoin escrow sales too.

Use a wallet made only for this market: this key lets the market see every address of that wallet account. In Bitcoin Core, listdescriptors prints lines such as wpkh([d34db33f/84h/1h/0h]tpub…/0/*): paste the part from [ up to /0/*. Never paste a private key (tprv), recovery words or anything from listdescriptors true.

Bitcoin Core keys only: required unless the key's path is the standard multisig path m/48h/1h/<account>h/2h. A per-order wallet key must be on that path.
