Take down listing

10–500 characters. Taking the listing down archives it and stops automatic delivery of its paid orders; the vendor can still edit it, but only an administrator can restore it. The vendor is notified with this reason and sees it on the listing; it is stored unencrypted and kept in both accounts' activity logs, never shown publicly. Never include an address, real name or tracking number.
