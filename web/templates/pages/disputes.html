Resolution center

Disputes.

Raise an issue with a paid, shipped, or delivered order. Market staff review it; on an escrow order only an administrator who is not a party decides it. An escrow order can be disputed until its deadline: a shipped order until its release deadline, and a delivered digital order during its 48-hour dispute window from delivery. After that the order completes and a dispute can no longer be opened.
