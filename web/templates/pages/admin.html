Administrator

Get your marketplace ready

  1. Protect your administrator account with two-factor sign-in.
  2. Add your public PGP key, then verify ownership so users can contact you securely.
  3. Check Bitcoin and Monero wallet status and choose which currencies accept new payments. Only test networks are supported.
  4. Publish your first listing, or invite a user to register and assign them the vendor role below.
  5. Assign a trusted moderator below and ask them to add and verify their PGP key for dispute messages.
  6. Open the marketplace in another browser profile to test the buyer journey.

Wallet connections and encrypted backups are configured on the server. This control room does not install nodes or expose their RPC credentials.
