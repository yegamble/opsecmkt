Get your marketplace ready

  1. Protect your administrator account with two-factor sign-in.
  2. Add your public PGP key, then verify ownership so users can contact you securely.
  3. Check Monero escrow status and choose whether to accept new Monero payments. {{if $.LiveNetwork}}This market is on mainnet: payments are real funds.{{else}}This market is on test networks.{{end}}
  4. Publish your first listing, or invite a user to register as a vendor and decide their application under Vendors: Vendor applications.
  5. Assign a trusted moderator and ask them to add and verify their PGP key for dispute messages.
  6. Open the marketplace in another browser profile to test the buyer journey.

Wallet connections and encrypted backups are configured on the server. This control room does not install nodes or expose their RPC credentials.

Assign an account role

Enter the handle exactly (it is case-sensitive) and choose buyer, vendor or moderator, or find the account in the user directory above and change its role on its page. The first administrator is created during installation; administrator roles cannot be assigned or changed here. The operator adds and removes administrators on the server with the -set-role host command. Choosing vendor here makes the account a vendor at once, recorded as an approved application and closing any open one; applications themselves are decided under Vendors: Vendor applications.
