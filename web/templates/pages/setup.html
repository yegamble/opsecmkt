Initialize your market.

Your application and database are ready. Finish installation by naming your marketplace and creating its administrator.

  1. Copy the setup token from your private deployment configuration.
  2. Choose the marketplace name and your administrator credentials below.
  3. Continue to the control room to configure payments, publish listings and invite users.

Installation runs once. The setup token proves you control this deployment; it is not your account password. Payments stay unavailable until a test-network wallet is configured.

Use at least 12 characters and at most 72 UTF-8 bytes. No email address is required.
