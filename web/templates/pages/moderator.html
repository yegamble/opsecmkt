Marketplace operations

Moderation desk.

Review disputes and record an outcome. Every open dispute is listed, oldest first. Payments flagged by the payment watcher are listed under Payment review.

Resolving a dispute records a release or refund outcome and closes the order. Resolving queues one payout of the whole counted amount to one party; the payment watcher sends it automatically. There is no split. Each outcome states the amount, who receives it and how it differs from the price. You cannot resolve disputes on orders you are party to.

Payment review

TESTNET deposits the payment watcher flagged for staff review: a credited deposit that became conflicted or missing or fell back below the confirmation threshold, a locked transfer, a deposit confirmed after the order's single release or refund, or deposits whose release or refund is below the minimum automatic payout. Open the order to read its history and deposits (read-only). Nothing here moves funds; see “Handle a payment-review flag” in docs/testnet-runbook.md.
