Moderation desk.

Review disputes and record an outcome. Every open dispute is listed, oldest first. Payments flagged by the payment watcher are listed under Payment review.

Resolving a dispute records a release or refund outcome and closes the order. This desk moves no funds itself; any payout or refund is handled by the payment system. You cannot resolve disputes on orders you are party to.

TESTNET deposits the payment watcher flagged for staff review: a credited deposit that became conflicted or missing, a locked transfer, or a deposit confirmed after the order's single release or refund. Open the order to read its history and deposits (read-only). Nothing here moves funds; see “Handle a payment-review flag” in docs/testnet-runbook.md.
